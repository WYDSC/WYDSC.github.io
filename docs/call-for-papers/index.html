Call for Papers

Explore the promises and risks of AI, and what they mean for human life, through humanism, science, religion and beyond.

An invitation to scholars.

Sogang University invites proposals for the 2027 WYD-Sogang International Conference: AI and Human Flourishing, taking place on 3–5 August 2027.

Amid the coexisting promises and perils of artificial intelligence, the conference examines the theme “AI: humanism, science and religion.” We welcome submissions that analyze the risks and benefits of AI through these fields, including multidisciplinary approaches that bridge them.

Themes & topics.

The following topics offer starting points for proposals. Contributions need not be limited to these areas.

01

Humanism

How can AI serve people and the societies we share?

  • AI and Future Governance
  • Peace and Democracy in the Era of AI
  • International Politics and Human Security in the Age of AI
  • AI, Labor Markets, and the Distribution of Gains
  • Markets, Firms, and Institutions in the AI Era
  • AI and Socioeconomic Development: Growth, Divergence, and Measurement
  • AI in University Education
02

Science

What can AI help us discover, build and care for?

  • AI for Healthcare: Medical Imaging, Diagnostics, Drug Discovery, Biomedical Engineering, Medical Devices, and Digital Health
  • AI for Sustainability: Renewable Energy, Environmental Protection, Advanced Materials, Carbon Neutrality, and Sustainable Manufacturing
  • AI for Advanced Technologies: AI Semiconductors, Robotics, Intelligent Systems, Smart Manufacturing, Autonomous Systems, and Next-Generation Electronics
  • Frontiers of AI and Intelligent Systems
  • AI for Scientific Discovery and Innovation
  • AI for Human and Planetary Well-Being
03

Religion

What does it mean to be human in an age of AI?

  • AI and the Definition of Personhood
  • AI and Spiritual Direction
  • Catholic Education in the Age of AI
  • AI from the Perspective of Catholic Social Teaching (or Moral Theology)
  • AI and the Catholic Understanding of the Human Person
+

Open track

Proposals beyond the three primary domains are welcome, including diverse perspectives and multidisciplinary work addressing AI and human flourishing.
